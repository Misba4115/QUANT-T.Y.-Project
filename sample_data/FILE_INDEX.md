## 📊 CSV Data Files (7 files)

### 1. **banking_accounts.csv**
- **Purpose**: Bank accounts and cash positions
- **Records**: 4 accounts
- **Total**: ₹950,000
- **Includes**: Savings, current, FD, RD accounts
- **Fields**: account_type, account_name, institution, balance

### 2. **equities.csv**
- **Purpose**: Direct stock holdings
- **Records**: 10 stocks (NSE/BSE)
- **Total**: ₹1,850,000
- **Includes**: RELIANCE, HDFCBANK, INFY, TCS, WIPRO, etc.
- **Features**: Mix of gains (+10.5%) and losses (-3.2%)

### 3. **mutual_funds.csv**
- **Purpose**: Mutual fund investments
- **Records**: 10 schemes
- **Total**: ₹2,350,000
- **Includes**: Equity funds, hybrid funds from major houses
- **Features**: NAV-based P&L calculations

### 4. **etfs.csv**
- **Purpose**: Exchange-traded funds
- **Records**: 10 ETFs
- **Total**: ₹750,000
- **Includes**: Index ETFs, gold ETFs, debt ETFs
- **Features**: Diversification across asset classes

### 5. **crypto_holdings.csv**
- **Purpose**: Cryptocurrency holdings
- **Records**: 10 cryptocurrencies
- **Total**: ₹650,000
- **Includes**: BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, XRP, etc.
- **Features**: High volatility examples (+33%, +25%)

### 6. **transactions.csv**
- **Purpose**: Bank transaction history
- **Records**: 43 transactions (3 months)
- **Period**: March 2026 - May 2026
- **Includes**: Income, expenses, investments, transfers
- **Analytics**: Monthly income ₹6L, expenses ₹2L, surplus ₹4L

### 7. **goals.csv**
- **Purpose**: Financial goals tracking
- **Records**: 8 goals
- **Total Target**: ₹376,000,000
- **Types**: House, retirement, education, emergency, wedding, wealth, etc.
- **Horizons**: 2026-2040 (short to long-term)
