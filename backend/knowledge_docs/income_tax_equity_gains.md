# Educational summary: equity capital gains

This is a curated educational summary, not tax advice. The source authority and effective date in the manifest must be shown with any answer that uses it.

For assessment year 2025-26, the Income Tax Department guidance identifies the section 112A threshold for eligible long-term equity capital gains as ₹1.25 lakh. Calculations must distinguish realized gains from unrealized holdings, establish acquisition and sale dates from transaction or lot records, and select rules by the applicable financial year. A holding creation timestamp is not evidence of acquisition date.

QUANT must not classify every equity or mutual-fund holding as an eligible section 80C investment. Eligibility requires an explicit product or transaction classification and supporting evidence. When purchase lots, holding periods, or eligibility evidence are incomplete, the application should mark the estimate unavailable and list the missing inputs rather than infer them.
